Jungle Pond with Cranes

Two long-legged cranes stand at the edge of a still jungle pond, one wading at the water, framed by an intensely dense rainforest of ferns, palms, and a lone orange bloom. The composition is edge-to-edge and busy, worked in deep emerald, moss, and sage greens with the pale reflective water opening the lower centre. The birds anchor the foreground and the pond forms a calm focal void, so centre that waterline on the wall and keep a low headboard or bench beneath it rather than over the cranes. A continuous scene, never tiled, and being child-safe and serene it suits a nursery, kids' room, bedroom, or living room. Soft Matte complements its soft, layered painterly surface.
