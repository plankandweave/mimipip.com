KIDS’ WALLPAPER

Mangal (मंगल)

Molten rivers of colour pour across this abstract, warm golds, oranges and reds swirling together while pools of bright turquoise break through like scattered gems. The fluid, marbled style carries real movement and energy, glossy and lively from edge to edge. With no fixed focal point, it crops cleanly to any proportion and forgives furniture placed anywhere across it, so a sofa or bed needs no careful alignment. Its cheerful, high-energy palette works in a kids' room, playroom, living room or a bright home office. Soft Matte complements the organic, poured character of the artwork. Produced as one continuous mural, scaled to your exact wall rather than repeated.
