Heron Fan Palm Garden

A serene grey pigeon perches on the lower right amid a verdant jungle, where a bright chartreuse fan palm bursts overhead and small yellow blossoms dot a still pond to the left. Detailed and gently stylised, the artwork stays almost entirely in rich greens lifted by the acid-yellow palm and warm bamboo trunks. The bird and radiant palm hold the right foreground, so on a wider wall the calmer pond and deeper forest extend leftward. Place any tall furniture on the left to keep the bird and palm crown clear. Its playful yet calm nature suits a nursery, kids' room, bedroom or living room, designed as one continuous mural. Soft Matte enhances its soft foliage detail.
