Tropical Birds Waterfall Vista

A misty jungle vista opens onto a distant waterfall framed by towering palms, with a crimson bird in flight and two perched birds — one red-capped, one soft dove-grey — anchoring the right foreground, plus a coral bromeliad at lower left. Warm cream light glows through the haze against muted greens, giving a fresh yet serene feel. Detail concentrates on the right and edges while the airy centre stays open, so a bed or sofa placed centrally leaves the birds visible. Lovely in a bedroom, nursery or living room. Its atmospheric brushwork calls for Soft Matte. Each panel prints as one continuous scene, not a repeat.
