Tropical Jungle Panorama

A lush tropical jungle scene layered with banana fronds, palms and broad foliage in deep and sage greens, punctuated by a jewel-toned bird — blue head, orange breast — perched beside clusters of amber-orange blossoms. The mood is earthy and richly detailed, dense from edge to edge, with the bird and flowers sitting low-centre. Scaled to a wide wall the canopy extends naturally, though a headboard or sofa could obscure the bird, so keep the lower-centre band clear or centre furniture below it. Ideal for a bedroom, nursery or living room. Soft Matte suits its painterly canvas character. This is a single continuous mural, sized to your wall rather than repeated.
