Snowlight Meadow

A silver-birch wood at dusk: small white hares in the snow, chickadees on the branches, and warm amber lights glowing among the frosted grasses, with a river winding to the mountains beyond. A cosy storybook world with little discoveries at a child's eye level. Painted with a soft gouache-and-watercolour feel, and sized to your wall.
