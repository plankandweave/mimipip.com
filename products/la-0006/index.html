Dense Tropical Fern Forest

A dense tropical valley unfolds in layers of palm fronds, broad banana leaves and feathery ferns, with misty blue hills receding behind and small red and yellow blooms tucked into the deep green foliage. The palette is lush emerald and jade against a soft cream sky, richly detailed and edge-to-edge busy in an illustrative botanical style. Greenery fills the full height, so the scene crops well on almost any wall while the misty gap at centre gives the eye room to rest; a sofa or bed sits comfortably against the leafy foreground. Great for living rooms, bedrooms, nurseries and kids' rooms. Soft Matte enhances the organic, layered leaves. Each mural prints as one continuous scene made to your wall's size, not a repeating pattern.
