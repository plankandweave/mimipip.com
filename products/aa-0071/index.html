Rekha – Dusty Rose Geometry

Loose brushstroke bars angle and intersect across a warm cream ground, forming an open geometric composition in dusty rose, deep burgundy, coral pink and soft taupe-grey. The mood is calm and airy, with generous negative space keeping it minimal rather than busy. The crossing lines gather toward the upper and right edges, so as the scene stretches to wider or taller walls the empty cream simply breathes outward while the strokes stay as gentle anchors. A low headboard or console along the base sits comfortably below the linework without hiding it. Well suited to a bedroom, nursery, teen room, home office or foyer. Rekha suggests a line or stroke, fitting the drawn geometry. Choose Premium Non-Woven for its refined matte finish that keeps these flat colour edges crisp. Designed as one continuous scene scaled to your wall, never a small repeating tile.
