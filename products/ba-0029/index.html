Tropical Parrot Leaves

Vivid tropical parrots perch and take flight among sage banana leaves, golden pineapples and looping gold chains, all set on a clean white ground. Emerald, teal and cobalt plumage flashes with orange and yellow accents for a playful, fresh energy, and the composition is busy from edge to edge. Birds and pineapples repeat across the field, so scaling or cropping keeps lively subjects in view and furniture hides nothing crucial. It works beautifully in kids' rooms and nurseries, and adds spirit to foyers or bedrooms. Designed as one continuous mural fitted to your wall, never a small tile. Premium Non-Woven suits its crisp, graphic tropical imagery and clean white background.
