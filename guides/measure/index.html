A good fit starts with your wall.

Use a tape measure and record the dimensions before choosing the final composition.

  1. Width: measure across the top, center and bottom. Keep the largest measurement.
  2. Height: measure left, center and right from the top of the baseboard to the ceiling. Keep the largest measurement.
  3. Record accurately: note your units. One foot is 12 inches or 30.48 cm. Round up rather than down.
  4. Map obstacles: note doors, windows, outlets and furniture. Do not subtract these areas from a continuous mural without checking the layout.
  5. Check the proof: confirm where the picture starts, where the joins fall and how the design is cropped. Confirm fitting allowance before production.

For sloped ceilings, record the enclosing rectangle and a sketch. For multiple walls, measure each wall separately and mark corners. A room photograph alone cannot establish accurate dimensions.

The product planner calculates wall area only. It does not generate a production proof, panel plan or final price.
