Will it work on your wall?

The wall underneath matters as much as the design on top.

Tell us about the surface

  • Smooth or textured: include orange-peel, knockdown or heavy plaster texture.
  • Paint type, finish and when it was applied.
  • Any peeling, flaking, dust, damp or previous wallcovering.
  • Whether the space gets steam or direct splashes.

Check the exact material

The wallpaper catalog records finishes such as smooth matte and linen feel. The final material, adhesive and fitting instructions must match your chosen design.

We do not make a site-wide PVC-free, low-emissions or GREENGUARD claim. Ask for product-specific evidence where that matters to your selection.

Fresh paint and textured walls

Follow the paint manufacturer's full cure time and the wallcovering supplier's preparation requirements. Dry-to-touch is not proof that a wall is ready. Do not assume adhesion to textured, chalky or damaged finishes; request a material test first.

Bathrooms and floors

Standard wallpaper is not intended for direct shower exposure. Decorative wall stickers and wet-area floor products have different requirements. Little Ocean's adhesion and grip checks must be completed before release.

Renting?

Check permission and the existing paint condition. Removal results depend on the finish, preparation and material; no universal damage-free or reusable promise is made.
