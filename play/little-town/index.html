Let’s play Little Town.

Drive to the shop, visit the park and find a parking place. Play with a finger or a favourite age-appropriate toy car.

Keep the floor clear and dry. Check that the sticker edges are flat. Choose movements that feel comfortable and let children take turns.

A little delivery

  1. Choose a finger as your car, or use an age-appropriate toy car.
  2. Start at the house. Follow the road to the shop, pretend to collect something, then drive home.
  3. Next time, visit the tree park before coming home. Take turns inventing deliveries.

Find a parking place

  1. Start anywhere on the road. Follow a full loop, then steer into one of the three bays.
  2. Reverse out carefully and try a different bay.
  3. Finish after visiting all three. No timer or racing is needed.

Tell a town story

  1. One player starts: “We left home to go to the park…” and moves along the road.
  2. The next player adds what happens next and moves to another destination.
  3. Continue for six turns, then bring everyone home. With one child, a parent can tell the next part.
