Meet the artist

Nafas Elizabeth Pattrick

Born into a world of adventure, Nafas began her journey before her first breath. While still in her mother’s womb, she travelled through forty countries across Africa, Europe and Asia on her parents’ motorcycle journey.

Today, at seven, she brings the confidence and curiosity of a well-travelled soul to her drawings. She loves being in front of the camera and making videos, and meets every creature she finds with kindness, eager to learn from the living world around her.

Pink Pop and Ruby & Snow are two of the faces she drew in colour; Face Parade sets them side by side. Then she picked up a pencil and drew her animals — a puppy, a fish, and a panda with a bird singing on its head.

Her colours, and her pencils

Pink Pop, cherry, sprinkle yellow, blue, green, coffee bean, latte and icing — alongside graphite, soft lead, smudge and paper.
