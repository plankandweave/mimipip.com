A few things worth knowing first.

Practical answers for a nursery, toddler room or small refresh.

Can I order now?

Not yet. Browse the collections, save designs and contact us for product information. No payment or order is taken online.

Are these wall stickers or wallpaper?

Both formats are presented, with separate categories. Wallpaper covers a measured wall; stickers are individual motifs. Compare formats.

Will they work on textured walls?

Compatibility depends on the exact material, paint and texture. Do not assume that a peel-and-stick product works on orange-peel, knockdown or rough plaster. Check the surface guide.

Is every wallpaper self-adhesive?

No. A matte finish is not an adhesive specification. Check the material and fitting method for the chosen design.

Does a nursery tag mean certified child-safe?

No. Stage tags help browse designs. They do not establish material certification, emissions testing or an age rating.

How large are the designs?

Wallpaper is planned around wall measurements. Little Ocean has a piece-by-piece dimension table. Room illustrations show possible styling; use product dimensions for planning.

Can I add a name?

You can try a name badge visually. It is a concept preview, not a production proof or an ordered customization.

Can I move or reuse it later?

Do not assume reuse or damage-free removal. Paste-the-wall wallpaper is intended for long-term installation. Ask about the exact material before choosing it for a rental.

Do you serve the US and India?

The website supports both browsing markets. Read regional shipping information and confirm a quote before planning installation.

Can I get a sample?

Ask about availability for the design and country. India wallpaper samples use a ₹200 reference service; international samples are not confirmed. Sample information.

Can decorative stickers go on the bathroom floor?

No. Decorative stickers must not be substituted for a floor-grip material. The separate Little Ocean floor version is awaiting material and wet-area performance validation.

Can I compare favorites with my partner?

Save designs on the homepage, or share the product link. Saved designs stay in the current browser; a shared catalog URL preserves filter choices but not your private shortlist.
