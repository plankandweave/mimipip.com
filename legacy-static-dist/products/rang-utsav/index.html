KIDS’ WALLPAPER

Rang Utsav (रंग उत्सव)

Layered washes of hot pink, magenta, coral, tangerine and chartreuse sweep across the surface with cool blue accents at the left, a celebratory blaze of colour fitting the name Rang Utsav, festival of colours. Bright and joyful, the strokes spread edge to edge with a subtle brighter cluster centre-left, yet no detail is so precise that furniture would spoil it. A headboard, sofa or console can sit anywhere against the even field. Cheerful for a kids' room, bedroom, living room or a boutique or salon. Each panel is one continuous mural scaled to your wall, not a small repeat. Soft Matte complements the soft, blended painterly layers.
