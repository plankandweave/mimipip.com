Anuraag (अनुराग)

Meaning “Love” or “Affection” in Sanskrit, symbolizing connection and harmony. This dazzling wallpaper depicts two beautifully adorned birds resting under a vividly detailed tree. Surrounded by blooming flowers and lush greenery, the design captures the essence of Madhubani art with its rich textures and vibrant palette. The warm hues of the sunset background bring an enchanting glow, adding warmth and character to any space. Ideal for spaces seeking an intimate and culturally rich ambiance.
