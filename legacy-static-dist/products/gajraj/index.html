KIDS’ WALLPAPER

Gajraj (गजराज)

( Meaning “King of Elephants,” symbolizing strength, wisdom, and royalty. ) This regal Madhubani-style wallpaper showcases a majestic elephant adorned with intricate floral and geometric patterns. The dark blue background highlights the vibrant colors of the elephant, including bright yellows, reds, greens, and whites, representing festivity and grandeur. The border is decorated with blooming flowers, adding a touch of elegance and charm. This design is perfect for creating a bold and cultural statement in spaces like living rooms, offices, or event spaces.
