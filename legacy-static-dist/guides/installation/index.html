Prepare well. Enjoy the room longer.

Use the fitting instructions supplied for the exact material. This overview helps you plan the work.

Before installation

Check the artwork, dimensions and panel sequence before cutting or hanging. The wall should be sound, smooth, clean and dry. Resolve damp, loose paint and uneven areas first. Keep tools and loose backing away from children.

Paste-the-wall wallpaper

For a confirmed paste-the-wall finish, apply the specified paste to the wall in manageable panel-width sections, hang the dry numbered panel, align the design and smooth outward. Follow the supplied join and trimming instructions. Do not use this method for self-adhesive decals.

Decorative stickers

First check material-specific surface and preparation instructions, then plan the arrangement. Do not stretch artwork to match a room illustration. Little Ocean application and cleaning instructions will be finalized after material testing.

Cleaning

For the sourced matte wallpaper care guidance, use a barely damp soft cloth gently; avoid abrasive scrubbing and harsh cleaners. Check the product's instructions before cleaning other finishes.

Removal

Long-term paste-the-wall wallpaper may need dampening before peeling. Removed panels should not be expected to be reusable. Paint condition affects the result. Follow material-specific removal guidance for stickers.
