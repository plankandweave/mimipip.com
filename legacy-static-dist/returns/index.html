Know the details before you choose.

Ordering is not yet open. These made-to-order wallpaper terms are adapted from Zyvorah and will be shown with the final MimiPip order terms.

Made-to-order wallpaper

Request cancellation within 12 hours of ordering and before printing begins. Once printing starts, cancellation and change-of-mind returns are unavailable because the artwork is printed to your measurements.

Measurements and color

Check your dimensions and approved composition carefully. Screens vary; slight differences from the printed color are expected. A printed sample is the best way to assess a color before ordering.

Damage or a print fault

Report transit damage within 48 hours, or a manufacturing fault within 7 days of delivery. Include the order reference and clear photographs of the product and packaging. Retain the packaging and avoid installing an affected panel until support has assessed it.

For a valid claim, the affected panels are reprinted or replaced. If reprinting is not possible, the resolution may be a credit or refund. Installation damage is separate from a manufacturing fault.

Stickers and floor games

Their return eligibility and procedures will be confirmed before these products are released. Do not assume that a custom-wallpaper policy also governs a ready-size sticker set.
