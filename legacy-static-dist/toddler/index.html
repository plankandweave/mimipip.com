Before you choose

  • Measure around furniture and leave the room's working areas clear.
  • Save a few designs to compare with another adult. Your shortlist stays in this browser.
  • Keep decorative wall stickers separate from floor products. Wet-area grip performance requires its own testing.
  • Use floor-game activities according to the child's abilities, with adult supervision; walking alternatives are included in the play guides.
  • Confirm wall preparation, sample availability and delivery before planning a birthday or room reveal.

Toddler tags describe room inspiration, not an age rating or a safety test result.
