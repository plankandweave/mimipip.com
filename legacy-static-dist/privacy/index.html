This page describes the current browse-and-plan website.

Saved designs and country

Your selected market and saved designs are kept in this browser's local storage when available. They are not an account and do not sync across devices. Remove saved designs in the saved-design panel or clear the site's storage in your browser.

Name preview

A name typed into the wallpaper badge preview is used on the current page. It is not included in the saved-design record or analytics events. It does not create a print file or personalization order.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you accept optional analytics. It measures page visits and design exploration. Our tracking does not include name-preview entries or enquiry text. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice.

Email and external content

Contact links open your email app. Your email provider handles sending, and our support mailbox receives what you choose to send. Product imagery, fonts and existing collection media may load from third-party hosts, which receive normal network request information.

Orders

No checkout address or payment details are collected by this website. A complete commerce privacy notice will accompany the order service when it opens.

For questions about your data, contact hello@mimipip.com.
