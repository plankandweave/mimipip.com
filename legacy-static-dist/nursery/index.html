Your nursery planning checklist

  1. Pick the wall and record its full width and height, including areas behind furniture.
  2. Check the paint finish, texture, age and any damp patches before choosing a material.
  3. Ask for a printed sample where available. Screen colors and room illustrations are not a substitute for the actual print.
  4. Confirm the final crop, panel layout, material and arrival estimate before scheduling installation.
  5. Keep loose backing, tools and any detachable decorative pieces away from children. Decorative stickers are not toys.

Nursery is a design-browsing category, not a product certification. Review the exact material and intended surface for your chosen item.
