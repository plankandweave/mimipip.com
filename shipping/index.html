Plan the room. Then plan the arrival.

MimiPip ordering is not open yet. The wallpaper planning terms below are adapted from Zyvorah; confirm the final quote and destination before arranging installation.

United States

For the sourced made-to-order wallpaper service, allow up to 5 working days for production, followed by an indicative 5–18 working days in international transit depending on destination. These are estimates, not a guaranteed arrival date.

International wallpaper delivery is included in the country-adjusted USD quote. Destination duties, local taxes and customs charges are separate and the recipient's responsibility. US wallpaper prices still need to be configured on MimiPip.

India

Standard wallpaper: dispatch within 5 working days, then typically 2–4 working days in transit. Standard delivery is included; wallpaper has a 20 sq ft billing minimum and 4 ft minimum chargeable width.

Express dispatch: within 2 working days, at ₹1,000 per wallpaper plus ₹500 per order. This accelerates production and dispatch, not courier transit. Festivals and long weekends can affect timing.

Stickers, floor games and samples

Wallpaper delivery terms do not automatically apply to these categories. Final material release, stock, dispatch and delivery charges will be confirmed separately before ordering opens.

Before you commit

Ask for the dispatch date, transit estimate, ZIP or PIN-code coverage, total price and any destination charges. Wait for delivery and inspect the product before booking installation.
