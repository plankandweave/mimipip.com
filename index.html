The MimiPip wallpaper standard

Made for children’s spaces, chosen with care.

Every wallpaper we list for sale must meet our material and indoor-air criteria. We’ll show product-level details only after the supporting documentation is confirmed.

01

Designed for kids

Patterns and stories selected for children’s rooms and family homes.

02

Self-adhesive

Self-adhesive construction is a requirement for every wallpaper offered here.

03

PVC-free

Material composition must be verified as PVC-free before listing.

04

UL GREENGUARD

Wallpaper listings must carry verified UL GREENGUARD certification for low chemical emissions.

These are MimiPip’ listing requirements. UL GREENGUARD certifies low chemical emissions; it does not mean zero emissions. The copied Zyvorah catalogue does not include product-level GREENGUARD, PVC-free or emissions evidence, so verify each sellable wallpaper before making those claims.
