# Zyvorah presentation rules adapted for MimiPip

These rules carry over Zyvorah's merchandising discipline while keeping MimiPip's own rounded typography and playful, parent-facing identity.

## Product imagery

- Use a product's explicitly mapped flat artwork or its rendered room scene from that product's catalogue record.
- Prefer a room scene for collection cards and product tiles; keep the flat artwork available in the product gallery as the exact design reference.
- Do not substitute unrelated room photography, decorative motifs, generic category icons, or an image from a different design.
- Keep the image caption and alt text tied to the exact design and room shown. If a mapped image is unavailable, show an honest unavailable state rather than another product's image.

## Palette and typography

- Keep page surfaces predominantly white, with warm ivory for occasional soft sections.
- Use MimiPip orange for primary actions and emphasis, warm brown for text, and pale blue only as a small accent.
- Keep Nunito for the storefront interface; the supplied wordmark remains artwork and is not re-typeset.
- Avoid introducing a separate palette for each collection. Let the product artwork carry its own colours.

## Collection integrity

- A result row belongs to one named collection. Never place unrelated collections side by side in the same row.
- Use only collection names that exist in product source tags. A product row's title, count, filter and image must all point to that same source collection.
- Do not invent a collection by grouping products only because their colours or themes look similar.
- Products without a named subcollection remain under the source Kids Wallpapers collection.

## Source preservation

- Preserve Zyvorah's original product names, artwork mappings, descriptions and source prices in the copied catalogue.
- These display rules do not change Zyvorah's source listings or imply that product materials and safety claims have been independently verified.
